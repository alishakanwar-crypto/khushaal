Question Paper Maker

Type or paste the paper, press Check for errors, then Save as PDF. Everything stays on this computer — nothing is uploaded.

1  Paper details

2  Questions

How to type it: SECTION A – Reading (10 marks) for a section heading · 1. or Q1. to start a question · (a) / (i) for parts · put the marks at the end in brackets like [2] or (2 marks) · a blank line leaves a gap.

3  Check & print

Save as PDF: the print window opens — choose Destination: Save as PDF, paper size A4, and switch Headers and footers off. To print copies for the class, choose the printer instead.

The checker catches mechanical and structural mistakes — spacing, punctuation, numbering, marks that do not add up, repeated questions, common misspellings. It is not a substitute for reading the paper: it cannot tell you that an answer is wrong or a question is unfair.
